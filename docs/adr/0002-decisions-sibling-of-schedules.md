# Keep time schedules and labeled decisions as separate objects

JRNY Plan's core object is a schedule of candidate times, with calendar overlap and a host-chosen final time. Labeled questions such as what to eat share host, participant, invitation, and signed-in public access, but they are not candidate times. Decision is a sibling that may stand alone or attach to one schedule, rather than making schedule options hold arbitrary labels or running a second invite system with no link to the event.
